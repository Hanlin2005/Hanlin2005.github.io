Spoken Arabic Digit Recognition

December 2024

Tools: Python, scikit-learn, SciPy

I classified spoken digits from 0 to 9 on the Spoken Arabic Digit dataset. Each utterance is represented by cepstral coefficients, and a Gaussian mixture model scores which digit those coefficients belong to.

The mixture parameters come from maximum likelihood, with K-means and expectation-maximization as the fitting methods I compared. The best run reached 90.64% accuracy. The work is written up in a 20-page report.
