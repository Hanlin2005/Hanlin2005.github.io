Variational Quantum Eigensolver

Tools: Python, Qiskit

I simulated ground-state estimation for Heisenberg models with IBM Qiskit, and compared a one-dimensional hardware layout with a two-dimensional one.

A circuit that does not match the connectivity of the device has to insert SWAP gates. I modelled that gate error and found that the topological mismatch adds a clear amount of noise. Among the optimizers I tried, SPSA held up better than COBYLA once the circuit was noisy.
