Solar Dryer for Duke Campus Farm

December 2023

Tools: client communication, technical documentation, testing, woodworking

The dryer at the farm. Herbs sit on the racks, and the glazed collector in front warms the air that passes through the cabinet.

I designed and built a solar herb dryer for the Duke Community Farm. The dryer is indirect. Sun falls on a tilted collector out front, and the herbs sit in a cabinet behind it, out of the direct light. Warm air from the collector moves up through the racks.

The cabinet is a wooden box on legs, painted green, with a plywood door and racks for the herbs. The collector is a glazed slope with a dark absorber underneath. In the photo the door is open and a few leaves are already on the trays.

The farm was the client. I had to settle what they needed dried, write the design down, build the box, and test that herbs actually dried in it. The dryer was finished in December 2023.
