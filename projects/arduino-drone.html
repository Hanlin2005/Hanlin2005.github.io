Arduino-Based Drone

May 2023

Tools: Arduino, Fusion, 3D printing, sensor integration

The quadcopter on the bench. The frame is printed, and the flight electronics sit in the center of the arms.

I designed a small, cheap quadcopter. The frame is four blue arms printed from a Fusion model, with an orange propeller and a motor at the end of each arm. An Arduino is the controller, and the sensors and motor wires meet in the middle of the frame.

The photo is the airframe before it is cleaned up. Cardboard and tape hold the center stack together, and the power and signal leads are still routed across the top. That was enough to mount the motors, the sensors, and the board on one rigid body.

The aim was a quadcopter I could design, print, and wire myself. The airframe in the photo is where that stood in May 2023.
